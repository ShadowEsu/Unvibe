# CMO email policy (Unvibe)

## Resend — product mail (marketing site)

Only these automatic or founder-ordered classes:

1. Thank you for waitlisting (confirmation)
2. Thank you for buying / plan change when productized
3. Thank you for giving feedback
4. Major updates the founder explicitly tells CMO to send

Do **not** email the same waitlist people over and over with nurture drips unless the founder orders a named major update.

Cash referral rewards are **real policy** — keep product/referral copy honest; verify before reward confirmation emails.

Beta invites remain human-gated CLI batches.

## AgentMail — acquisition

Weekend builder outreach only. See skill `cmo-builder-outreach`. Max 20/day Sat–Sun. Personalized research required. Separate from Resend waitlist list.
