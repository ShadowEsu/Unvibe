## Voice rules

- Short sentences. Plain verbs. Sentence case.
- Write from the reader's side of the screen. They select code, they pick a depth.
- Never use: revolutionize, unleash, seamless, supercharge, game changer, delve, testament, tapestry, journey, elevate, empower, harness, robust, cutting edge, at the end of the day.
- Never use the rule of three as a rhythmic crutch. If a sentence has three parallel clauses, cut one.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Ever. Comma, colon or a new sentence.
- No rhetorical question openers. No "But here's the thing". No "It's not X, it's Y" construction.
- Buttons say exactly what happens. Join the beta produces a waitlist confirmation that says Joined the waitlist.
- Errors explain what broke and what to do. They do not apologize.
- Empty states invite an action.
- Never claim scale Unvibe does not have. It is in private beta and the copy says so proudly.

## Tone

Confident and slightly wry, aimed at someone who has pasted code they do not understand and feels a little bad about it. The site's job is to remove the shame and hand them the tool, not to lecture.
