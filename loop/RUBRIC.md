# Rubric

Score 0 to 100. Weights fixed.

| Axis | Weight | Full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Fidelity | 25 | Token discipline is total. A Raycast designer would not flinch. |
| Signature | 20 | The Explain Sweep explains the product without a caption. |
| Copy | 15 | Every sentence earns its place. No banned vocabulary. No dashes. |
| Motion | 15 | Short, eased, orchestrated once. |
| Accessibility | 10 | Keyboard complete, contrast clean, reduced motion respected. |
| Responsive | 8 | Perfect at 390 and 1440. |
| Performance | 7 | No CLS, lean client JS. |

Any blocker caps the total at 70. Report per axis.
